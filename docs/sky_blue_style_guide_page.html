Headings and numbers: Bricolage Grotesque. Body text, buttons and forms: Figtree.

3. Buttons

Corners 26 px, bold text, padding 14 px by 24 px. One primary button per area.

Primary: moves up 3 px, glows and shines on hover, gets smaller when pressed. Still happening: click it to see it turn into the blue gradient.
